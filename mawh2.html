Make-A-Wish Hawaii Internship


Wish Bar Wrapper Design


Background

In the middle of my internship, I was given the task to design a wrapper for a chocolate bar that would be distributed for the purpose of bringing awareness to those who may be in a position to refer a child to Make-A-Wish. Aside from incorporating Make-A-Wish's aesthetic, I also had to incorporate food-themed elements and taglines that fit the branding.
This project was solely designed in Adobe Illustrator.
